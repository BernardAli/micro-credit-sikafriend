# Agent Manual

Detailed application procedures are in the [Dashboard and Application Operations Manual](../dashboard_operations.md).

## Purpose

Agents onboard and support borrowers, submit loan applications, monitor assigned portfolios, collect repayments, and follow up on arrears.

## Access boundary

Agents use `/login/` and are redirected to their employee/portfolio page. They may access only records assigned to their linked workforce profile. Attempts to update another agent's loan, payment, or collection are blocked.

## Daily workflow

1. Open My Dashboard and review assigned borrowers.
2. Check active loans and amounts due.
3. Prioritize overdue and high-risk accounts.
4. Record verified customer interactions.
5. Submit new borrowers or loan applications where permitted.
6. Record collections with accurate method and reference.
7. Review pending payments and resolve exceptions.
8. Check Notifications before signing out.

## Borrower onboarding

Collect accurate name, phone, location, identity number, photo and Ghana Card images, business or employment information, preferred language, and group information. Obtain customer consent before capturing or uploading personal data.

Search before creating a new record. Report suspected duplicates to an administrator.

## Loan application

Complete all required fields and explain terms to the borrower. Confirm that the borrower understands principal, repayment frequency, installment amount, duration, total expected repayment, declarations, guarantor obligations, and collateral conditions.

Submission sends the application to Loan Requests. Do not promise disbursement until the request is approved and executed.

## Repayment collection

- Count or verify funds in the customer's presence.
- Record the correct loan and amount.
- Select the actual payment method.
- Enter provider or receipt references accurately.
- Never backdate or split transactions to hide discrepancies.
- Give the customer confirmation through the approved channel.

Pending payments are not final until confirmed. Escalate incorrect, duplicated, reversed, or unmatched payments immediately.

## Portfolio management

Use the Portfolio tab and analysis pages to monitor total loans, active accounts, collections rate, current-month collections, outstanding balance, and PAR proxy. Follow up respectfully and document commitments.

## Data protection

Do not store borrower documents on personal messaging accounts or share account data with unauthorized people. Lock devices, protect passwords, and sign out when work is complete.

## Escalation

Contact an administrator or manager for suspected fraud, identity mismatch, missing funds, system access problems, disputed balances, deceased customers, legal complaints, or any request to alter historical financial records.
