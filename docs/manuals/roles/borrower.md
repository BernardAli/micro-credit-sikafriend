# Borrower Portal Manual

Detailed application procedures are in the [Dashboard and Application Operations Manual](../dashboard_operations.md).

## Purpose

The borrower portal lets a customer privately review their own loans, repayments, balances, statements, and payment options.

## Signing in

Open `/login/` and enter the username and password issued by SikaFriend. After login, borrowers are redirected to `/my-account/`.

Never share your password or verification information. SikaFriend staff should not ask for your password.

## Portal overview

The overview shows your loans and important account information. Select a loan to see principal, expected repayment, amount paid, remaining balance, status, schedule, and transaction history.

## Reviewing a loan

Check that your name, amount, repayment terms, payment history, and balance are correct. Contact SikaFriend promptly if a payment is missing or information appears unfamiliar.

## Making a payment

Open the relevant loan and select the payment action. Follow the displayed instructions and use the correct reference. A payment may remain pending while the provider or staff confirms it. Do not submit the same payment repeatedly because confirmation is delayed.

Keep the mobile money, bank, or receipt confirmation until the payment appears on your account.

## Changing your password

Use **Change Password** under My Account. Choose a password that is difficult to guess and is not reused on another service. Sign out after using a shared device.

## Getting help

Contact SikaFriend when:

- You cannot sign in
- Your phone number or personal details changed
- A payment is missing or duplicated
- Your balance appears incorrect
- You suspect unauthorized account access
- You need a statement or repayment explanation

Provide your name and loan reference, but never send your password.

## Privacy

The portal is limited to your own records. Do not attempt to access another customer's account. Report any unexpected information immediately and sign out.
