# Management Manual

Detailed application procedures are in the [Dashboard and Application Operations Manual](../dashboard_operations.md).

## Purpose and account setup

Management is a workforce classification with elevated governance responsibility. A management user must have:

- A linked workforce record with role **Management**
- Admin Console Access enabled
- A personal login account

Management users enter the administrator workspace but carry additional self-review authority.

## Governance authority

Management users may approve or reject requests they submitted themselves. This applies to loan, payroll, accounting, and asset workflows. The permission exists for small organizations where another reviewer may not be available.

Self-review is an exception, not the preferred routine. When another qualified manager is available, use independent review.

## Loan review standard

Before approving a loan request:

1. Verify customer identity and application completeness.
2. Confirm amount, term, frequency, installment, and total expected repayment.
3. Review borrower exposure and repayment history.
4. Review guarantor and collateral evidence.
5. Check the assigned agent and operational location.
6. Record a meaningful review note for exceptions or elevated risk.
7. Approve only when the facility is affordable and policy-compliant.

Approval executes the request and creates the loan. A failed execution must be investigated; do not assume a loan exists until the request status is Executed.

## Payroll, accounting, and asset approvals

For payroll, reconcile employee count, gross pay, adjustments, deductions, statutory amounts, and net total. For journals, confirm supporting documents and balanced debit/credit entries. For disposals, confirm authorization, asset condition, proceeds, buyer information, and accounting treatment.

## Management dashboard routine

- Review portfolio value, collections, PAR, and overdue exposure.
- Compare agent performance and collection rates.
- Review large loans, repeat borrowing, and concentration risk.
- Monitor liquidity, expenses, payroll, and asset position.
- Review audit events and failed workflows.
- Confirm regulatory and internal reporting completeness.

## Review notes

Good notes state what was checked, the decision basis, and any condition. Avoid vague notes such as “OK.” Example:

> Identity and business cash flow reviewed. Existing balance is current. Approved within Tier 2 limit with weekly repayment and guarantor confirmation.

## Security and accountability

Management and superuser accounts are high-risk accounts. Use strong passwords, individual accounts, secure devices, and immediate access removal after role changes. Every self-review remains visible in the audit trail.
