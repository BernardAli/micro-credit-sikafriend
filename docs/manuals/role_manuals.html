Common rules

  • Use only your own account.
  • Protect borrower and workforce personal data.
  • Verify financial transactions before confirmation.
  • Report errors instead of hiding or overwriting them.
  • Sign out on shared devices.
Role 1

Administrator Manual

Administrators run the back office and maintain the integrity of company, borrower, lending, payment, workforce, payroll, accounting, asset, and communication records.

Access and accountability

Administrators are Django staff or superusers and sign in at /login/. Each person must use a named account so audit events remain attributable.

Control: Ordinary administrators cannot approve their own submissions. Only management-linked accounts and superusers have that exception.

Daily operating sequence

  1. Review Dashboard totals, overdue exposure, and collection indicators.
  2. Review pending Loan Requests.
  3. Review payroll, accounting, and asset Approvals.
  4. Confirm valid Pending Payments and investigate reconciliation exceptions.
  5. Review Notifications and failed communication activity.
  6. Verify backups and sign out at close of business.

Borrowers and loans

Search for duplicates before onboarding. Verify identity, contact information, Ghana Card evidence, location, agent assignment, and customer consent. Loan submission creates a pending request rather than a loan. Before approval, review amount, term, installment, declarations, guarantor, collateral, affordability, and existing exposure.

Payments

Match borrower, loan, amount, method, date, and reference. Pending is not final. Force reconciliation only with independent provider evidence.

Workforce and payroll

Maintain employment, access, compliance, banking, and compensation data. Restrict Admin Console Access. Review employee count, earnings, allowances, commissions, adjustments, deductions, statutory amounts, and net totals before payroll approval.

Accounting, assets, and settings

Confirm balanced journals and supporting documents. Maintain asset acquisition, custody, value, maintenance, depreciation, and disposal evidence. Back up before major company-setting changes.

Security checklist

  • Disable leavers promptly.
  • Keep secrets out of notes.
  • Investigate failed approvals.
  • Protect identity documents.
  • Test backup restoration.
Role 2

Management Manual

A management user has a linked workforce record with role Management, Admin Console Access, and a personal login.

Governance authority

Management users may approve or reject requests they submitted. This is intended for small-company continuity and remains fully audited. Independent review should still be used when another qualified reviewer is available.

Loan review

  1. Verify identity and application completeness.
  2. Confirm amount, duration, frequency, installment, and total expected repayment.
  3. Review repayment history and exposure.
  4. Review guarantor and collateral evidence.
  5. Confirm agent and location.
  6. Record the decision basis in the review note.
Status matters: Approval creates the loan only when execution succeeds. Confirm the request is Executed; investigate Failed requests.

Other approvals

For payroll, reconcile totals and statutory values. For accounting, verify documents and balanced entries. For asset disposals, verify authority, condition, proceeds, buyer information, and accounting treatment.

Management routine

  • Monitor portfolio, collections, PAR, and concentration.
  • Compare agent performance.
  • Review liquidity, payroll, expenses, and assets.
  • Review audit activity and failed workflows.
  • Confirm reporting completeness.

Review notes

A useful note records what was reviewed, why the decision is reasonable, and any condition. Avoid “OK” without context.

Role 3

Agent Manual

Agents onboard borrowers, submit applications, collect repayments, manage assigned portfolios, and follow up on arrears.

Access boundary

Agents are restricted to their linked workforce profile and assigned records. Accessing or changing another agent's portfolio is prohibited and blocked by the system.

Daily sequence

  1. Review assigned borrowers, dues, and overdue accounts.
  2. Prioritize high-risk follow-up.
  3. Record accurate interactions and applications.
  4. Record verified collections with correct references.
  5. Resolve pending-payment exceptions with administrators.
  6. Review Notifications and sign out.

Borrower onboarding

Capture accurate identity, phone, location, documents, occupation or business, language, and group. Obtain consent and search for duplicates.

Loan applications

Explain principal, frequency, installment, term, total repayment, guarantor obligations, and collateral conditions. Submission enters Loan Requests. Never promise disbursement before approval and successful execution.

Collections

  • Verify funds in the customer's presence.
  • Use the correct loan, amount, method, and reference.
  • Never backdate or split payments to hide discrepancies.
  • Keep confirmation evidence.
  • Escalate duplicates, reversals, disputes, or missing funds.

Data protection

Do not retain customer documents in personal messaging accounts or share account data. Protect devices and passwords.

Role 4

Borrower Portal Manual

The portal provides private access to the borrower's own loans, repayment history, balances, statements, password, and payment initiation.

Sign in

Open /login/ with the username and password issued by SikaFriend. The portal opens at /my-account/. Staff should never request your password.

Reviewing your account

Select a loan to review principal, expected repayment, amount paid, balance, status, schedule, and transaction history. Report unfamiliar or missing information promptly.

Making a payment

  1. Open the correct loan.
  2. Select the payment action.
  3. Follow the instructions and use the correct reference.
  4. Keep the provider or receipt confirmation.
  5. Wait for pending confirmation; do not repeatedly submit the same payment.

Password and privacy

Use Change Password to choose a strong unique password. Sign out after shared-device use. The portal is limited to your records; report unexpected information immediately.

When to contact SikaFriend

  • Login problems
  • Changed personal details
  • Missing or duplicated payments
  • Incorrect balances
  • Suspected unauthorized access
  • Statement or repayment questions
Provide your name and loan reference when requesting help. Never provide your password.
Part II

Dashboard & Application Procedures

This section explains how the operational applications work and the steps users follow on the dashboard.

Using the dashboard

  1. Use the sidebar to select a module.
  2. Read flash messages after every action.
  3. On tabbed pages, open the relevant tab before entering data.
  4. Correct all items in an error summary.
  5. Confirm the success message and saved record before leaving.

Company and public website

  1. Open Company Settings.
  2. Complete legal, contact, registration, licence, branding, and logo information.
  3. Configure loan, repayment, statutory, email, SMS, and USSD settings.
  4. Complete public hero, about, services, team, branches, FAQs, and documents.
  5. Save, then verify the signed-out website at /.
Before major configuration changes: create and verify a database backup.
Application Procedures

Borrowers & Portal Access

Create a borrower

  1. Open Borrowers and search for existing name, phone, national ID, or Ghana Card.
  2. Select Add Borrower.
  3. Enter identity, contacts, location, business/employment, agent, group, and language.
  4. Upload authorized identity images.
  5. Enter a unique portal username when required.
  6. Submit, correct visible errors, and verify the detail page.

Edit and review

  1. Select the borrower from the directory.
  2. Use Edit for evidenced corrections.
  3. Review status, loans, payments, balances, and risk indicators.
  4. Use Borrower Analytics for portfolio patterns.

Create or reset portal access

  1. Open borrower details.
  2. Select Create Login if no account exists.
  3. Set a unique username and secure temporary password.
  4. Deliver credentials through an approved channel.
  5. Verify identity before Reset Login.
Do not create duplicates. Duplicate borrower identities distort exposure and collection reporting.
Application Procedures

Loans, Requests & Statements

Submit a loan application

  1. Open New Loan and select borrower and agent.
  2. Enter principal, duration, repayment frequency, dates, installment, sector, and purpose.
  3. Complete borrower declarations and signatures.
  4. Add guarantor and collateral data where required.
  5. Review calculated values and submit.
  6. Confirm the message states that a request was submitted.

The loan does not exist yet; it is now in Loan Requests.

Review a loan request

  1. Open Loan Requests and filter Pending.
  2. Review borrower, amount, term, repayment, agent, evidence, and risk context.
  3. Enter a meaningful note.
  4. Select Approve & Execute or Reject.
  5. Confirm Executed or Rejected. Investigate Failed requests.

View a loan

  1. Open All Loans and select the facility.
  2. Review status, terms, expected amount, paid amount, and balance.
  3. Open Statement or Statement PDF.
  4. Verify transactions before sharing.
Application Procedures

Payments & Reconciliation

Record a repayment

  1. Open the loan and select Add Payment.
  2. Enter amount, date, actual method, and reference.
  3. Submit and note whether the payment is posted or pending.

Confirm a pending payment

  1. Open Pending Payments.
  2. Match customer, loan, amount, method, and reference to evidence.
  3. Confirm valid payments or Reject invalid payments with a reason.
  4. Reopen the loan and verify its balance.

Reconcile online collections

  1. Open Payment Reconciliation.
  2. Compare client/provider references, status, amount, borrower, and loan.
  3. Prefer normal provider callback processing.
  4. Use Mark Successful, Force Reconcile, or Mark Failed only with evidence.
  5. Verify that one payment was created and the balance changed once.
Never submit the same collection repeatedly. Escalate uncertain provider status before forcing reconciliation.
Application Procedures

Workforce & Payroll

Create an employee

  1. Open Workforce and Add Workforce Member.
  2. Profile tab: code, name, role, department, supervisor, status, dates, and location.
  3. Contact & Compliance: contacts, address, emergency details, Ghana Card, tax, and SSNIT.
  4. Payroll: salary, allowances, commission, payment routing, payroll and access flags.
  5. Documents & Notes: upload authorized files and notes.
  6. Save and use the error summary if validation fails.
  7. Verify Overview, Payroll, and Portfolio tabs.

Create login

  1. Open the employee and select Create Login.
  2. Set unique credentials.
  3. Enable Admin Console Access only when authorized.
  4. Test the role's landing page.

Generate payroll

  1. Open Payroll > Run Setup.
  2. Add required earnings or deduction adjustments.
  3. Enter run title, period, pay date, options, and notes.
  4. Generate Draft Payroll and review every line.
  5. Recalculate if source records changed.
  6. Approve according to workflow.
  7. Mark paid only after disbursement; export for reconciliation if authorized.
Application Procedures

Accounting

Initial setup

  1. Open Chart & Cashbook.
  2. Create ledger accounts and bank/mobile money cashbooks.
  3. Create counterparties under Receivables & Payables.
  4. Create periods and budgets under Controls & Budgets.

Sync operations

  1. Open Accounting Overview.
  2. Select Sync Loans, Repayments & Payroll Into Ledger.
  3. Review the result and Recent Ledger Activity.
  4. Inspect Trial Balance; investigate differences before repeating sync.

Post accounting records

  • Cashbook: choose account, date, direction, amount, reference, and description.
  • Journal: enter header and balanced debit/credit lines.
  • Invoice/Bill: choose counterparty, dates, amount, status, and mapping.
  • Budget: create header, then add account lines.

Reports

  1. Open Accounting > Reports.
  2. Set start and end dates and Refresh Reports.
  3. Review Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, Cashbook, and Budget vs Actual.
Application Procedures

Assets

Register

  1. Open Assets > Register.
  2. Create a category where required.
  3. Enter code, name, category, acquisition date/cost, depreciation data, location, and status.
  4. Save and verify in Recent Assets.

Custody and maintenance

  1. Open Custody & Maintenance.
  2. Select asset and employee, enter dates, condition, and notes; Record Assignment.
  3. For maintenance, record provider, dates, cost, work, and outcome.

Depreciation and disposal

  1. Open Depreciation & Disposal.
  2. Set period/run date and Post Depreciation.
  3. Review generated lines and ledger effect.
  4. For disposal, enter asset, date, proceeds, buyer, reason, and accounts.
  5. Submit and complete the request in Approvals.
Application Procedures

Communications & Marketing

Send messages

  1. Open Messages.
  2. Choose recipient group and filters.
  3. Write the message and Preview Recipients.
  4. Verify count, identities, and normalized phone numbers.
  5. Select Send Messages only when correct.

Campaigns

  1. Save reusable content under Marketing > Templates.
  2. Under Campaigns, choose channel, audience, filters, content, and scheduling data.
  3. Preview Audience.
  4. Verify recipients and content, then Launch Campaign.
  5. Review History for outcomes.

Leads and popup

  1. Open Leads & Popup.
  2. Review website leads and update status.
  3. Configure popup content, image, CTA, dates, and active state.
  4. Save and verify on the signed-out website.
Application Procedures

Reports, Governance & Backup

Reports Center

  1. Select report type and period type.
  2. Select month, quarter, half-year, year, or custom dates.
  3. View Report and verify scope and totals.
  4. Export only after validating the on-screen result.

Approvals

  1. Open Approvals for payroll, accounting, and asset requests.
  2. Filter and review the full request context.
  3. Add a decision note.
  4. Approve & Execute or Reject.
  5. Confirm final status and result.

Audit Trail

  1. Search by record, action, object, or message.
  2. Filter category.
  3. Review actor, timestamp, action, record, and message.
  4. Preserve evidence for investigations.

Database backup

  1. Open Database Backup and select scope.
  2. Refresh Scope and review the overview.
  3. Download the required format to protected storage.
  4. Record operator, date, scope, and verification.
  5. Test restoration periodically outside production.
Application Procedures

Borrower Portal & Troubleshooting

Borrower portal

  1. Sign in and open My Account.
  2. Select a loan to review terms, payments, balance, and statement.
  3. Use Make Payment and keep provider evidence.
  4. Wait for pending confirmation; do not duplicate submissions.
  5. Use Change Password and sign out.

When an action fails

  1. Read the flash message and form error summary.
  2. Confirm required fields and active tab.
  3. Confirm the account role permits the action.
  4. Check Pending, Executed, Rejected, or Failed status.
  5. Avoid duplicate submission.
  6. Record route, time, reference, and visible error.
  7. Ask an administrator to review Audit Trail and server logs.
Never edit the database manually to hide an operational failure. Correct the cause, preserve the audit history, and verify the resulting balances.

Close-of-day control

  • Verify collections and pending payments.
  • Review pending and failed requests.
  • Review overdue follow-up.
  • Verify backup completion.
  • Sign out.
