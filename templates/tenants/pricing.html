Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

Every plan includes a full-featured trial — 14 days for Starter & Professional, 30 days for Enterprise. No credit card required to start.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is my data isolated from other companies?

Completely. Each company gets its own dedicated PostgreSQL schema — your data is never mixed with another organisation's records.

Do you support multiple currencies?

Yes. Each tenant configures their own currency, symbol, and locale from the company settings panel.

What payment methods do you accept?

Currently GHS bank transfer and mobile money. We're adding card payments — contact us if you need an alternative arrangement.

How do I get support?

Email support is included on all plans. Professional and Enterprise customers get priority response within 4 business hours.
