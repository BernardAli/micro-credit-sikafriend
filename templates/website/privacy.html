Information We Collect
01

We collect information that is necessary to evaluate loan applications, manage accounts, prevent fraud, and comply with financial regulations. This includes:

Personal Identification Data
  • Full legal name, date of birth, and national identification number (Ghana Card, Passport, or Voter ID)
  • Residential address, GPS coordinates, and proof of address documents
  • Photograph and biometric data where legally required for identity verification
  • Next-of-kin information and guarantor details
Financial Information
  • Income details, employment status, employer information, and payslips
  • Bank account numbers, mobile money wallet numbers, and transaction history
  • Existing loan obligations and credit history from credit bureaus
  • Business financial statements and trading records (for business loans)
Technical & Usage Data
  • IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, and navigation patterns on our website
  • Communication records including phone calls, SMS, email, and WhatsApp messages
We only collect information that is necessary, relevant, and proportionate to the purpose for which it is collected, in accordance with applicable data protection laws.
⚙️
How We Use Your Data
02

Your information is used solely for legitimate business and regulatory purposes:

Purpose Details
Loan Processing Evaluating creditworthiness, verifying identity, and making lending decisions
Account Management Setting up and managing your borrower account, repayment schedules, and statements
Collections Sending repayment reminders, contacting guarantors, and managing overdue accounts
Regulatory Compliance Meeting obligations under the Bank of Ghana, Anti-Money Laundering (AML) laws, and credit reporting regulations
Fraud Prevention Detecting and preventing fraudulent applications, identity theft, and financial crime
Service Improvement Analysing trends to improve our products, processes, and customer experience
Communications Sending loan confirmations, statements, policy updates, and relevant product information

We will not use your personal data for purposes incompatible with those stated above without first obtaining your explicit consent.

⚖️
Legal Basis for Processing
03

We process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Contractual necessity — processing required to fulfil the loan agreement between you and {{ profile.company_name }}
  • Legal obligation — processing required to comply with financial sector laws, anti-money laundering regulations, and reporting obligations
  • Legitimate interests — fraud prevention, risk management, portfolio analytics, and business security, where these interests do not override your rights
  • Consent — for optional communications such as newsletters or marketing messages; you may withdraw consent at any time
🤝
Data Sharing & Disclosure
04

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the following circumstances:

Regulated Third Parties
  • Credit Bureaus — We report loan performance to licensed credit bureaus as required by the Credit Reporting Act. This helps build your credit history and may affect your ability to access credit from other lenders.
  • Bank of Ghana — Regulatory submissions, AML/CFT reports, and supervisory disclosures as required by law.
  • Law Enforcement — In response to a valid court order, subpoena, or lawful government request.
Service Providers
  • Mobile money and payment processors used to disburse funds or collect repayments
  • SMS and communication service providers used to send account notifications
  • Cloud hosting and IT service providers who process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements
Business Transfers
  • In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred to the successor entity, subject to the same privacy protections.
All third parties with whom we share data are contractually required to maintain its confidentiality and use it only for the specified purpose.

Data Retention
05

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy and to comply with our legal obligations:

Data CategoryRetention Period

Your Rights
06

Subject to applicable law, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure — Request deletion of your data where there is no legitimate reason for us to continue processing it
  • Right to restrict processing — Request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes
  • Right to withdraw consent — Withdraw consent for optional processing at any time without affecting prior lawful processing
To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section. We will respond within 30 days. Some rights may be limited where we have overriding legitimate interests or legal obligations.

Data Security
07

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Encrypted storage of sensitive financial and identification data
  • Role-based access controls ensuring staff can only access data relevant to their function
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure HTTPS connections for all web-based communications
  • Staff training on data protection and confidentiality obligations
  • Physical security controls at premises where paper records are stored

In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the relevant regulatory authority as required by law.
