The Process

Get funded in four simple steps

We have streamlined the lending process so you spend less time on paperwork and more time growing.

1

Register

Visit us or contact your assigned agent with your national ID and basic documents to open your borrower account.

2

Apply

Choose your loan amount and duration. Your agent or our team will guide you to the right product for your situation.

3

Get Approved

We verify your application and notify you within hours. Approval is based on need, capacity, and good standing.

4

Receive Funds

Funds are disbursed directly to your mobile money or bank account — typically within 24 hours of approval.
