Our Story

Built for the micro-credit industry

We built {{ saas_name }} because we saw how many micro-credit institutions were still managing hundreds of borrowers in spreadsheets. There had to be a better way.

Why we exist

Micro-credit institutions deserve professional tools

Across Africa, thousands of micro-credit companies, savings groups, and cooperative lenders are growing rapidly — but still relying on paper ledgers and Excel spreadsheets to manage millions of cedis in loans.

{{ saas_name }} was built from the ground up for this space. Not adapted from corporate banking software. Not a generic loan tracker. A purpose-built platform that understands micro-credit — group loans, field agent collection, weekly repayments, and the realities of lending in African markets.
