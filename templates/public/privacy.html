Privacy Policy

Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %} · Applies to all {{ saas_name }} users and visitors

We do not sell your data. We do not share your Customer Data with third parties for advertising. Your organisation's data is stored in an isolated schema — other tenants cannot access it.

1. Who We Are

{{ saas_name }} is a Software-as-a-Service platform for micro-credit institutions. When we say "we", "us", or "{{ saas_name }}", we mean the {{ saas_name }} service and its operators. Our contact email is {{ saas_email }}.

2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Account and Registration Data

  • Your name, email address, and company name when you sign up.
  • Billing information (handled securely via our payment processor — we do not store raw card numbers).
  • Communication preferences and account settings.

2.2 Customer Data (Your Borrowers, Loans, Agents)

All data you enter into {{ saas_name }} about your borrowers, loans, agents, and transactions is "Customer Data". You own this data entirely. We process it only to deliver the Service to you. Customer Data is stored in an isolated PostgreSQL schema assigned exclusively to your organisation.

2.3 Usage and Technical Data

  • IP address, browser type, and device information when you access the Service.
  • Page views, feature usage, and error logs for platform improvement and debugging.
  • Session data stored in encrypted browser cookies required for authentication.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the {{ saas_name }} platform.
  • To authenticate you and protect your account security.
  • To send transactional emails (account confirmations, invoices, password resets).
  • To respond to support requests and inquiries.
  • To analyse aggregate, anonymised usage patterns to improve the product.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your Customer Data to train machine learning models, sell to advertisers, or share with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Isolation and Security

Each organisation's data is stored in a dedicated PostgreSQL schema. This means your borrower records, loan data, and financial information is not stored alongside other organisations' data in shared tables. Schema-level access controls enforce that only your organisation's users can access your schema.

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encrypted database storage at rest
  • Regular automated backups
  • Strict access controls and audit logging for platform administrators
  • Role-based access control within your organisation's workspace

5. Data Sharing

We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use trusted third-party services for hosting, email delivery, and SMS. These providers are contractually bound to protect your data and may only use it to provide services to us.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of {{ saas_name }} or others.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, Customer Data may be transferred. We will notify you before this occurs and you may request deletion.

We will never sell your Customer Data.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate personal data via your account settings or by contacting us.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated Customer Data. We will action this within 30 days.
  • Export: Request an export of your Customer Data in a standard format (CSV/JSON) at any time.
  • Objection: Object to our processing of your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at {{ saas_email }}.

7. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies for authentication and session management. These are required for the platform to function and cannot be disabled. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your subscription is active. After account cancellation or trial expiry, Customer Data is retained for 90 days to allow data export, then permanently deleted. Aggregated, anonymised usage statistics may be retained indefinitely.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email at least 14 days before they take effect. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
