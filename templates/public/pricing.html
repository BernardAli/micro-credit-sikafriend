Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?
Every plan includes a full-featured free trial — 14 days for Starter & Professional, 30 days for Enterprise. No credit card required to start. You get access to every feature in your chosen plan from day one.
Can I switch plans after signing up?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle. Your data is never deleted on a downgrade.
Is my company's data isolated from others?
Absolutely. Each company gets its own dedicated PostgreSQL schema. Your borrower records, loan data, and financials are never mixed with another organisation. This is not shared-table multi-tenancy — it is true schema isolation.
What currencies does the platform support?
Each tenant configures their own currency, symbol, and decimal format from the company settings panel. The platform is used with GHS, NGN, KES, UGX and other African currencies out of the box.
What happens when my trial ends?
Your account enters a grace period. You can continue to access your data and reports for 7 days after the trial expires. After that, the account is suspended until a subscription is activated. Your data is retained for 90 days.
Do you offer discounts for NGOs or cooperatives?
Yes. We offer special pricing for registered NGOs, cooperative societies, and savings groups. Contact us at {{ saas_email }} with your registration details to learn more.
